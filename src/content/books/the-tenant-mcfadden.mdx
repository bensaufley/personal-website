Solid thriller. The first half was extremely stressful, really driving home the "someone is ruining this character's life" storyline with very little reprieve and IMO not a \_lot\_ of hints about why. I had a hunch early that turned out to be correct, but I had abandoned that hunch by the time it all unfolded, which is I think the sign of a good novel of this type.<Spoiler>The "bonus" twist at the very end, to me, served no purpose and didn't really add anything except to try to hit the reader with "absolutely nothing here was as it seemed," but it's fine.</Spoiler>
