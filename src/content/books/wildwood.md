This has been on my list forever. I finally bumped it up because of the movie. It's good! I liked it. I have trouble understanding the scale of Wildwood. They drive around in cars, but you can get from one "country" to another in minutes on foot? And as a parent, the behavior of the kids' parents regarding their children is insane. Took me out of it. And the noble bandits are regularly robbing farm laborers? Seems less than noble. But it's a fun children's fantasy world. I may pick up another in the series at some point and I'll definitely watch the movie.
