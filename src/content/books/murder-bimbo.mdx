This wasn't quite what I expected it to be – the blurb had me thinking there'd be a bit more … righteousness? But I definitely enjoyed it. It was more thought-provoking than the book I expected to read might have been. I found the ending a bit unsatisfying, because<Spoiler>it undermined – for me – any remaining sense that I was rooting for "the good guy" _and_ any sense of even a compromised happy ending</Spoiler>; but that left me thinking about it all a lot more than a more straightforward "eat the rich" thriller might have.
